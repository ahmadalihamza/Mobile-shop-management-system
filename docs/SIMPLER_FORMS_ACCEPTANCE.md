# Purchase phone names and reason-free entry — Mobile Desk 1.6.0

The purchase list must identify the phones bought without requiring the owner to open every batch. Its Products / phones column uses immutable purchase descriptions, including purchases entered in older versions. A purchase containing several products remains one purchase row and displays all distinct product descriptions. A product appearing in several stacks or exact-paisa cost portions is listed once. Search, date/type/supplier filtering, pagination and original purchase amounts retain their existing behavior.

Manual reason entry is removed throughout the UI: supplier shipment return/cancellation, customer sale return, customer/supplier balance adjustment, latest-entry undo, selected purchase undo, and sale/payment/expense/transfer/adjustment/customer-return corrections. Confirmation dialogs remain for corrections; password protection remains for owner actions. Services accept omitted/blank reasons and record factual action descriptions automatically. Optional custom descriptions from existing integrations remain compatible. Existing stored reasons are preserved rather than deleted or rewritten.

Tests verify multiple saved phone names, cost-allocation deduplication, historical naming after product edits, filters/pagination, reason-free operations, owner authentication, stock/financial dependency guards, automatic audit details, and absence of reason-entry controls in actual Qt forms. The full existing business suite and Windows source/packaged application flows must pass. Additional form simplifications are documented separately for the owner's decision and are not applied in this release.
