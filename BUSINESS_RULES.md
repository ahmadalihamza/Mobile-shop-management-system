# Trading rules — 1.3.0

- Quantity inventory, no IMEI requirement. A catalogue reference and a shop product are independent.
- Money is integer paisa. RMB price/rate are exact decimals; converted PKR unit cost rounds once to paisa, then quantity multiplies that cost. Additional PKR cost per unit is included in landed stock cost.
- Purchase bills use converted unit price. Carry enters the supplier bill only when explicitly billed by that supplier. Separate carry payments reduce cash/bank, never profit a second time.
- China carry is entered as one total per stack. The charge is converted once at its saved rate and divided across the pieces without losing a paisa; original input and exact totals are retained. Local purchases and pre-upgrade records retain their recorded PKR-per-piece carry. Average Purchase Price is weighted net acquisition landed cost per purchased piece, including awaiting-arrival purchases and excluding supplier returns/cancellations. FIFO sale costs remain original allocations.
- Awaiting-arrival purchases create supplier bills/actual payments but no saleable stock. Physical arrival adds only uncancelled pieces once. Owner-authenticated full/partial cancellations and stock returns retain originals, issue supplier credit and remove eligible available stock; confirmed supplier/carrier refunds are separate dated cash receipts. Unconfirmed claims never create cash. Returned external carry not credited by the supplier is a cost loss until an actual carrier recovery is recorded.
- Opening stock creates inventory at a verified cost, with no bill or supplier debt.
- FIFO uses original posted lots, within the stock chronology rules. Each sale records immutable allocated unit costs and product/party descriptions.
- Saving sales, purchases, returns and payments uses a single SQLite write transaction. Invalid stock, party, date, account, amount or cost aborts the entire operation.
- A customer is required for unpaid sales. An unpaid purchase in the UI requires a supplier. Fully paid walk-in transactions need no contact.
- Customer ledger: sales plus opening/adjustments minus receipts and returns, plus refunds. Supplier ledger: non-opening supplier bills plus supplier opening/adjustments minus supplier payments. Roles never net against one another implicitly.
- Each partial/later payment is a separate dated record. Payments/advances on account settle oldest unpaid bills for the account balance view. Attached invoice receipts and returns stay with the original document. Original payment history is not rewritten.
- Gross profit is net sales minus original allocated cost. Period profit deducts recorded shop expenses and explicit customer discounts. Purchases, supplier payments, collections and account transfers do not create extra revenue/profit.
- Returns reference an actual invoice and restore the original allocations. Return quantities cannot exceed unreturned pieces. Walk-in returns require full refunds. Normal returns are for saleable stock.
- Corrections void the original with an automatically recorded action description and reverse related stock/payments. Operators do not enter reasons. Older descriptions remain intact. Posted dependents prevent unsafe stock reversal. Replacement entries are new records. Later on-account payments remain valid ledger entries/advances.
- Purchase Undo requires owner credentials and is blocked once return history exists. Payments supporting returns/refunds cannot be voided. Finalized return/refund histories are immutable, with owner/reason/quantity/credit/refund audit evidence.
- Backups are checksummed consistent snapshots, restored only after application/schema/integrity/FK verification. Restore protects the previous databases. The catalogue is included but stored independently.
- Production never seeds Excel data. The explicit authorized clean-start utility archives current databases before replacing business data; it preserves catalogue bytes and shop settings and legitimate account configuration, resets account openings and invoice sequence. It never runs automatically.
- Retired Excel backups/startup databases are rejected. Schema upgrade compatibility fields/tables remain empty for clean production and do not provide an import UI or automatic migration path.
- Undo Last Entry reviews the most recent active supported transaction in audit save order and uses the same dependency-aware audited reversal as selected corrections. No reason entry is required. It does not erase original records, change product/settings edits, or reverse later independent payments.
- Owner reset requires username/password and literal RESET confirmation. A verified consistent safety backup must succeed before staging a fresh business database. Directory replacement rolls back if validation fails or startup detects an interrupted swap. Shop identity, account names, catalogue and owner credentials remain; all business entities, openings and invoice sequence reset. Normal monthly work retains stock and unpaid balances.
- Owner passwords use salted scrypt hashes, constant-time comparison and a persistent one-minute delay after five failed attempts. Credentials live outside replaceable business databases; resets/restores cannot roll them back. Changing a configured password requires the current one. A damaged credential file blocks these actions. Windows file access is governed by Windows permissions; the business databases and backups are not encrypted.
